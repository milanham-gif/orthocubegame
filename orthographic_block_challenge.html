How to Play & Learn

Objective:

Learn spatial geometry by building 3D wooden cube houses and comparing them to their 2D orthographic projections (Top, Front, and Side views).

Building Controls:

  • Add Block: Select "Add Block" mode and click on grid cells or adjacent side faces.
  • Remove Block: Select "Remove" mode, or Right Click directly on any block.
  • Rotate Camera: Right-click and drag (full 360° rotation allowed).
  • Zoom: Scroll mouse wheel or pinch.

Puzzle Solved!

Awesome spatial thinking! Your 3D house matches all three 2D projections perfectly.
